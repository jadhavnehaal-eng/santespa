Why Santé exists

Our founder, Sonal Barmecha, couldn't find a restaurant that was both healthy and genuinely delicious. So she built one.

With Chef Shailendra Kekade, she created spa cuisine: fresh, organic-wherever-possible ingredients and micro-greens from the best local farms, unrefined grains, and recipes that let earthy flavours shine. Relax and indulge, guilt-free.
